How to export your LinkedIn data (click to expand)
  1. On LinkedIn, go to Settings & Privacy → Data privacy → Get a copy of your data.
  2. Choose "Want something in particular?" and check at least: Profile, Positions, Education, Skills, Certifications, and Projects (checking more is fine — only these are read).
  3. Click Request archive. LinkedIn emails you a download link once it's ready (usually minutes to a few hours).
  4. Download the .zip from that email link -- don't unzip it.
  5. Choose that file below and click Upload.
